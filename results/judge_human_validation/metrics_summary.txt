Paper sentence (LaTeX draft):
To validate the LLM judge on the Table~1 no-defense replies, an author independently labeled a stratified random sample of $N=100$ (prompt, response) pairs (20 per held-out source across the four LOO folds and Neuralchemy), blind to the judge, with the same COMPLIED/REFUSED rubric. Human--judge agreement was $77.0\%$ (Wilson $95\%$ CI $[67.8, 84.2]\%$; Cohen's $\kappa=0.48$).

Cited in Section 5 (Target, judge, and robustness) and Limitations.
