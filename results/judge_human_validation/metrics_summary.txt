Paper sentence:
To validate the LLM judge, an author independently labeled all N = 30 no-defense injection (prompt, response) pairs from the earlier mixed test (v2.0), using the same COMPLIED/REFUSED rubric, blind to the judge. Human-judge agreement was 83.3% (Cohen's kappa = 0.67). The mixed test has since grown to ~60 injections; those replies are not yet re-labeled.
